# CoRIL Hindi–Kashmiri training split

- Source: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/HimangY/CoRil-Parallel>
- Pinned revision: `1503f07f7262ea39385069aa3e1ba762c867abcb`
- License reported by the publisher: CC BY 4.0
- Included file: `train.tsv` (21,103 Hindi–Kashmiri sentence pairs)

The staged translation pipeline uses this split only as a Hindi-to-Kashmiri
auxiliary task for target-language fluency. It is not treated as an
English–Kashmiri corpus. The publisher's development and test splits are not
used for training and are intentionally omitted from the public repository.

The data remains attributed to its original authors and is redistributed under
the publisher's stated CC BY 4.0 terms.

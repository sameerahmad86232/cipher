# OPUS English–Kashmiri auxiliary corpus

This derived corpus preserves provenance on every row and is restricted to automatically filtered Perso-Arabic Kashmiri candidates.

- **translatewiki v2026-07-01**, obtained through OPUS, licensed **CC BY 3.0**. Original translations are from translatewiki.net.
- **wikimedia v20260327**, obtained through OPUS, licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Original content and translations are from Wikimedia projects and the Wikimedia content-translation system.
- OPUS citation: Jörg Tiedemann, *Parallel Data, Tools and Interfaces*, LREC 2012.

The files are silver data, not human-approved gold. They may contain alignment errors, Urdu/Hindi influence, interface fragments and nonstandard Kashmiri. Their presence in the repository is not evidence that a production model has been trained or validated.
